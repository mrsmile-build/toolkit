Why Simplify Your Text?

Simple language reaches more people. Research shows that content written at a simple reading level gets 3x more engagement than complex text. Whether you are writing for customers, students or social media, simpler text always wins.

Nigerian Pidgin is understood by over 75 million people across Nigeria and West Africa. Use it to connect with a wider local audience on social media, WhatsApp broadcasts and marketing materials.
