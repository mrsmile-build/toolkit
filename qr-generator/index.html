What is a QR Code and How Does It Work?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that smartphones can scan to instantly open a website, show contact information, connect to WiFi or display any text. QR codes are used on business cards, product packaging, restaurant menus, marketing materials and more.

Best Uses for QR Codes in Business

Add a QR code to your business card linking to your website or Selar store. Print QR codes on flyers to drive traffic to your digital products. Use QR codes on product packaging to show instructions or warranty information. They are free to generate and instantly scannable.
