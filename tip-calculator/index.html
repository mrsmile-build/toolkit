How to Calculate a Tip

To calculate a tip, multiply your bill amount by the tip percentage. For a 15% tip on a $50 bill, multiply 50 by 0.15 to get $7.50. To split the total between people, divide the total including tip by the number of people.

Standard Tip Percentages by Service

Restaurant servers typically receive 15 to 20 percent. Delivery drivers receive 10 to 15 percent. Hotel housekeeping receives $2 to $5 per night. Taxi and rideshare drivers receive 10 to 15 percent. Use this calculator to split bills fairly every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

The standard restaurant tip is 15 to 20 percent of the pre-tax bill. For excellent service, 20 to 25 percent is appropriate. Use our tip calculator to find the exact amount instantly.

How do I split a bill equally?

Enter the bill amount, tip percentage and number of people in the calculator. It will show exactly how much each person pays including their share of the tip.
