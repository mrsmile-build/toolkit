How to Write a Good Caption

A good caption grabs attention in the first line, delivers value or emotion in the middle, and ends with a clear call to action. This AI caption generator does all three automatically based on your product and chosen tone.

Caption Tips by Platform

Instagram works best with 3-5 sentences plus hashtags. TikTok captions should be short and punchy under 150 characters. WhatsApp business captions work best with emojis and a direct offer. LinkedIn needs professional language without slang.
