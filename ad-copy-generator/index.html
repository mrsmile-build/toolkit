What Makes a Good Ad?

The best ads speak directly to one person's pain or desire. They have a strong hook in the first line, clear benefits in the middle, and a specific call to action at the end. Avoid vague claims — be specific about what your product does and for whom.

For Nigerian audiences, use relatable language, mention local context where relevant, and always include a direct call to action like "DM me now" or "Click the link in bio".
