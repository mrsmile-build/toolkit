How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage calculator solves three common problems instantly — finding what percentage one number is of another, calculating percentage increase or decrease, and finding the original value after a percentage change. These calculations appear constantly in business, finance and everyday life.

Common Uses for a Percentage Calculator

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate percentage of a number?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example 25 divided by 200 multiplied by 100 equals 12.5%. Our calculator does this automatically.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. Enter both values in our calculator and get the result instantly.
