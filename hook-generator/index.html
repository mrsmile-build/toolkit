What is a Hook and Why Does It Matter?

A hook is the first 1-3 seconds of your video or the first line of your post. It determines whether people keep watching or scroll away. Studies show that 65% of viewers decide within 3 seconds whether to continue watching a video.

The best hooks create curiosity, make a bold claim, ask a compelling question or trigger an emotion. This generator creates hooks proven to stop the scroll based on your topic and chosen style.
