How to Price Your Product

The most common pricing method is cost-plus pricing — add your desired profit margin to your total cost. For physical products, aim for 40-60% margin. For digital products, aim for 70-90% margin since your cost after creation is near zero.

Always research competitor prices before setting yours. Price too low and customers question your quality. Price too high and you lose sales. Find the sweet spot where value justifies the price.
