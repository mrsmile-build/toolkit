How to Use Your Script

Once you have your script, paste it into VideoKit to generate an AI voiceover. Then record your screen or face while the script plays. Edit in VN Editor or CapCut — add captions, music and effects. Post on TikTok and Instagram Reels for maximum reach.

The best performing videos are 30-60 seconds with a strong hook in the first 2 seconds. Always end with a clear call to action like "Follow for more" or "Link in bio".
