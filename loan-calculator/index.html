How Does a Loan Calculator Work?

A loan calculator uses three inputs — loan amount, interest rate and loan term — to calculate your exact monthly payment and total interest paid. This helps you compare loan offers and understand the true cost of borrowing before you commit.

Tips to Reduce Your Loan Interest

The fastest way to reduce loan interest is to make larger monthly payments, choose a shorter loan term, or negotiate a lower interest rate. Even a 1% reduction in interest rate can save thousands over the life of a loan.

How is monthly loan payment calculated?

Can I use this for any type of loan?

Yes. This calculator works for personal loans, car loans, mortgages, business loans and any fixed-rate loan.
