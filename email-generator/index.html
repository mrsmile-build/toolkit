Tips for Writing Effective Business Emails

Keep your subject line under 50 characters and make it specific. Start with a personal opener, get to the point quickly and always end with a clear call to action. Avoid long paragraphs — use short sentences that are easy to read on mobile.

Follow-up emails have a 21% higher open rate than first contact emails. Always follow up at least once if you don't get a response within 3 days.
