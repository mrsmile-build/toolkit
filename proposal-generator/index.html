How to Write a Winning Proposal

A strong proposal focuses on the client's problem first, not your services. Start by showing you understand their situation, then explain how you will solve it, followed by your specific deliverables, timeline and price. End with a clear next step.

Keep proposals concise — one page is ideal for most freelance services. Follow up within 48 hours if you don't hear back. Most clients make decisions within 3 days of receiving a proposal.
