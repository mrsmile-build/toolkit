Why You Need a Strong Password

Weak passwords are the number one cause of account hacking. A strong password uses a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. It should be at least 12 characters long and never reused across multiple accounts.

How to Manage Your Passwords Safely

Generate a unique strong password for every account using this tool. Store them in a trusted password manager like Bitwarden or 1Password. Never write passwords in plain text or share them through messaging apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a password strong?

A strong password is at least 12 characters long and contains uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special symbols. Avoid using names, dates or dictionary words.

Is it safe to use a password generator?

Yes. This password generator runs entirely in your browser. No passwords are sent to any server or stored anywhere. Every password is generated locally on your device.
